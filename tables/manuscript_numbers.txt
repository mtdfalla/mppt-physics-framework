─── NOTE ON STATISTICAL TESTS ──────────────────────────────────────────
  Reviewer 2.11 requests t-tests, ANOVA, confidence intervals.
  RESPONSE: The simulation is fully deterministic — all parameters are
  fixed, there are no random seeds or stochastic elements. Re-running
  any case produces bit-identical results. Therefore inferential
  statistics (t-test, ANOVA) do not apply: there is no sampling
  variance to characterise. What is reported instead:
  - Distribution statistics across the 32 test conditions per algorithm
    (mean ± std of η_total, RMSE, MAE)
  - Per-case RMSE and MAE as absolute error metrics
  - std_P_ss as a measure of steady-state oscillation magnitude
  This approach is standard in deterministic PV simulation studies.
